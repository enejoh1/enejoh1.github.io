BVN Verification Portal

Cooperative Mortgage Bank · compliance team
PHP 8cURLPrembly IdentityPasszero dependencies

Problem

The compliance team needed to verify customers' BVNs. Every lookup costs money and returns a real person's name, date of birth, address and photograph. It also had to run on shared cPanel hosting with no Redis, no database and no build step.

What I built

A server-side proxy to Prembly, so the live secret key never reaches the browser. Each lookup is shown as a verification credential with the passport photo. Access is bcrypt-gated, config sits above the web root, and every lookup goes to an append-only audit log.

Outcome

Compliance staff verify identity in seconds through one audited tool. Credentials can't leak to the client, and a fault can't silently burn paid lookups.

Key decision: the rate limiter fails closed. With no Redis or database available, I wrote a file-backed sliding-window limiter, guarded by flock for concurrency, keyed on hashed IPs, and self-pruning so the store can't grow without limit. If the counter ever breaks, lookups are blocked rather than allowed. Failing open would have meant leaking paid identity credits.

Coopmoni: licensed digital banking

Coopvest Limited · one of three principal engineers
Laravel 10PHP 8.1MySQLAWSFlutter

Problem

Cooperative members needed real banking: loans, savings, transfers and collections, delivered under an FCCPC licence, with identity checks and audit trails a regulator can inspect.

What I built

The referral programme end to end (codes backfilled across existing users, bonus payouts, milestones, dashboard). Loan disbursement and reversal, 30-day settlement, offline repayment reconciliation and automated repayment reminders. Wema Bank virtual accounts with inbound webhooks. GL transfers and the maintenance-fee engine.

Outcome

A Laravel backend with 45 domain models serving a Flutter app live on the Play Store (build 69). Each member gets a dedicated account number for collections.

Key decision: a switchable identity-provider interface. I sourced and negotiated the Prembly partnership, then integrated it next to Adjutor behind one interface. If one vendor goes down, onboarding keeps working.

CMB Learning Management System

Cooperative Mortgage Bank · built solo
Laravel 13PHP 8.3TailwindVite

Problem

Staff training wasn't measured or tied to performance reviews. HR needed scores they could trust, by staff member and course, for each appraisal period.

What I built

14 domain models covering departments, courses, lessons, enrolments and assessments. A timed, auto-scored assessment engine with attempt limits, pass marks and question shuffling. Role-based separation of learners and admins, append-only audit logging, and an automated test suite. Frontend included.

Outcome

Training results now feed directly into HR appraisal, with an export trail that records who exported which figures and when.
